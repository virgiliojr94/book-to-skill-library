# Glossary — MrBeast Production

Alphabetical.

- **A/B/C Player** — talent buckets: A = obsessive/coachable/no excuses (the only type that stays); B = trainee headed to A; C = average/paycheck-only, transitioned out immediately (Ch 4)
- **AVD** — Average View Duration; time the average viewer watches; the core retention metric (Ch 1)
- **AVP** — Average View Percentage; fraction of a video watched; used to measure content goodness post-upload (Ch 1, 3)
- **Back half content** — the final minutes past minute 6; viewer is in a lull, explanations/long bits live here; never signal the end early (Ch 1)
- **Backup day** — one extra deliverable/rental day reserved because filming rarely lands on time/budget; also a debate to have with creative before locking sets (Ch 2)
- **Bottleneck** — the person/step that blocks your video from starting; must be told face-to-face, dated, and checked in on daily (Ch 2)
- **Brand deal as content** — a sponsor integrated into the video so retention doesn't crater; yields residual customers across years (Ch 3)
- **C-Player** — see A/B/C Player (Ch 4)
- **Clickbait** — the title/thumbnail promise; matching it is mandatory or viewers click off (Ch 1)
- **Communication lines** — the rule to communicate up-and-then-over through dept heads so options are known; skipping makes double/triple work (Ch 2)
- **Concept (render)** — an inspiration render that is NOT building-locked; must not be called a Pre Vis (Ch 2)
- **Consultants** — people who already solved your problem; use first on every task (Ch 2)
- **Creativity saves money** — the default answer to budget pressure: reframe, don't spend (Ch 2)
- **Critical Component** — something essential to the video (island, title/thumbnail, must-have challenge); treat as a baby, check in 10x/day, build a backup, never coin-flip it (Ch 2)
- **CTR** — Click Thru Rate; clicks ÷ impressions; the metric that dictates which videos get made (Ch 1)
- **Dump-and-forget** — delegating then ignoring until the shoot; the cardinal sin; the blame lands on YOU (Ch 2)
- **Excite me** — the goal of all content: must get Jimmy excited to film; predicted by what makes the audience happy (Ch 3)
- **Feasibility** — verifying a critical component is actually possible before committing to production (Ch 2)
- **Format** — the video's structural mold; two formats architect retention: stair stepping and payoff (last-to-leave) (Ch 3)
- **Higher form of communication** — in-person > call > text > email; the more complex/dangerous the message, the higher the form needed (Ch 2)
- **Information diet** — everything you consume (social, tv, youtube, games); the raw material for culture and jokes; monitor and perfect it (Ch 3)
- **Lull** — the back-half viewer state: watching without realizing they're watching (Ch 1)
- **Math/Science/Vision/Approvals/Budget** — the five levers that solve every problem (Ch 2)
- **Minute architecture** — first minute → minutes 1–3 → 3–6 → back half, each with a retention job (Ch 1)
- **No does not mean no** — pushing thru one person's "no" by exhausting all avenues (fans, bosses, social team, other branches) (Ch 2)
- **Payoff** — the satisfying end reward/flip that holds retention (last-to-leave reveals winner at the end) (Ch 3)
- **Pre Vis** — a render of what will actually be built; don't call concepts by this name (Ch 2)
- **Prios** — your assigned priority list; nothing (even helping others) may come before them (Ch 2)
- **Pushing thru no** — see No does not mean no (Ch 2)
- **Re-engagement** — a "only MrBeast can do this" spectacle segment at ~3 and ~6 minutes to re-hook the viewer (Ch 1)
- **Results-based / Results > hours** — the company judges output, never clock time (Ch 2, 4)
- **Say the negatives** — tell leadership why something is BAD, not just why it's good (Ch 2)
- **Stair stepping** — format that escalates stakes progressively (fireworks: $1 → $100k → record) with payoff at end (Ch 3)
- **Video everything** — recording sets/items for the whole team to reference, scaling one person's mental grip to the team (Ch 2)
- **Wow factor** — something no other YouTuber can do (house on a crane); untrackable but brand-defining (Ch 1)
- **"Out of 10"** — Youtube's view-ranking comparison vs the previous 9 uploads, hour by hour (Ch 2)
