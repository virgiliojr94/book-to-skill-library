# Cheatsheet — MrBeast Production

Decision rules — how Jimmy decides, one page.

## The North Star
- The goal is the BEST **Youtube** video possible — not best-produced, not funniest, not highest quality. Every other metric follows.
- The goal of content is to **excite Jimmy** — his predictable rule: what excites him = what makes the audience happy.

## The Three Metrics (measure virality)
| Metric | What | Decision lever |
|---|---|---|
| CTR | clicks ÷ impressions | dictates WHAT videos to make (title/thumbnail) |
| AVD | average seconds watched | governs minute architecture + first minute |
| AVP | % of video watched | judges content after upload (day-one data) |

## Minute Architecture (what minute are you editing? Avg video = 13:37)
| Zone | Job |
|---|---|
| First minute | Match clickbait expectations; good lighting; front-load interest (biggest drop-off happens here) |
| Minutes 1–3 | Hypet→execution: stop promising, start showing; compress progression to hook investment |
| ~3 min | Re-engagement: "only MrBeast can do this" spectacle |
| Minutes 3–6 | Simple, stimulating, fast cuts; win hearts of story/people |
| ~6 min | Second re-engagement, pushes into back half |
| Back half | Viewer in a "lull": explanations/long bits live here; NEVER signal the end except to hype the payoff |

## Retention Rules (always)
- Ends must be **abrupt** (protects retention). No slow fades.
- **No dull moments** the whole video. **No faked intensity**.
- Something goes wrong on set → check if it's content ("the tree that broke the 100 vases is hilarious").

## Formats Quick-Pick
- Want escalating stakes → **Stair Stepping** ($1 → $100k firework; payoff at end).
- Want engineered retention → **Payoff format** (last-to-leave, chase): hide the result till the end.
- NEVER run the same format twice in a row; rotate.

## Production Playbook (leave no room for error)
- **Bottleneck**: tell the owner face-to-face, agree a date, check in DAILY with video evidence.
- **Contractor**: never dump-and-forget; demand weekly marker deadline; daily calls/videos → know weeks ahead, not days.
- **Critical Component**: who/what essential? Protect like a baby: 10x/day check-ins, parallel back, no standard shipping, speak up the second something breaks. **Never coin-flip the video.**
- **Prios**: the assigned list is LAW; helping others can't come before it.
- **Always have a backup day** for expensive sets/tanks.
- **If problem:** ask "is this best for creative, or the easiest bandaid?" — creative first, always.

## Money & Creativity
- Creativity saves money — reframe the outcome: $20k prize → 1,825 packs of Doritos (~$1,825).
- **$10k+ spent that won't show on camera** → seriously re-think.
- Consultants = cheat codes: someone's already done it, use them first. Math/Science/Vision/Approvals/Budget solve everything.

## Communication Matrix (higher complexity → higher form)
| Situation | Channel |
|---|---|
| Trivial (TP restock) | text |
| Routine update | text/call |
| Critical component flounder, ~1 week out | minimum multi-way call with heads; ideally in person |
| Any written message | doesn't count until they confirm they read it |

## Corporate No-No's
- Excuses → 0 tolerance. Mistakes fine (every vet cost a million); **repeating** them = annoying.
- C-Players: transitioned out IMMEDIATELY. Stay A-Player: obsessive, coachable, zero excuses.
- "I'm in production, I'm not creative" = "I suck at my job."
- Don't take anything at face value — dig (10,000 pillows from the 11th company = investigate).

## Career Levers
- Results > hours — judged on output, never clock time.
- Reviews on demand: say the intent ("I want to be production manager"), ask why you suck + how to get better, master the list, get the shot.
- Growth = responsibility + risk + bullshit you sign up to handle; money follows. Not tenure, not title.
- Watch ~last 50 videos to be 60% up to speed; all the way to the 10M special for full history.

## Talent Care

- Do NOT talk down to talent; keep them happy and in good moods; most talent talks route via Jimmy/Ali/Tyler.
