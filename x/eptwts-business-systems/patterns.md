# Patterns

## Usefulness Test

**When to use**: Before publishing content, asking for access, or designing an offer.  
**How**: Name the recipient, the result they care about, the competence required, and the transferable benefit you will deliver. If the benefit is vague, deepen the skill or narrow the promise.  
**Trade-offs**: Delays self-promotion and may produce narrower content, but grounds authority in demonstrated value.  
**Sources**: [Jan 2](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2007091103229448465), [Aug 24](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2091925587434614963)

## Proven-Offer Apprenticeship

**When to use**: When a beginner wants to build a product but has not yet sold or distributed one.  
**How**: Pick a proven offer, sell it through an affiliate channel or direct service, record buyer language and objections, and delay product investment until a channel and repeated pain are visible.  
**Trade-offs**: Less ownership and status at first; lower simultaneous product/distribution risk and faster market feedback.  
**Sources**: [Jan 24](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2015038599406784869), [Jun 6](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2063197143834243082), [Jul 24](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2080583304416309707)

## Problem-Backed Learning Loop

**When to use**: When choosing what to learn or when study is delaying action.  
**How**: Choose a real problem, attempt it, isolate the next gap, learn the minimum needed, apply immediately, and use feedback to choose the next lesson.  
**Trade-offs**: Coverage is uneven and some theory is deferred; knowledge becomes tightly coupled to useful output.  
**Sources**: [May 2](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2050596641019707536), [Jun 19](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2067921418919063634)

## Surface-Area Portfolio

**When to use**: When several small opportunities could create different transferable skills.  
**How**: Bound the downside of each experiment, name the skill or relationship it could create, act quickly, and review both venture results and capability gained.  
**Trade-offs**: Breadth can become distraction without bounded experiments and explicit learning capture.  
**Sources**: [Jan 7](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2008906931671327172), [Jul 22](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2079838342628151586)

## Living Context Wrapper

**When to use**: When a generic agent repeatedly lacks project, user, or domain context.  
**How**: Put authoritative detail in portable files; add an index, operating rules, links, and a low-friction inbox; update during normal work; require retrieval before consequential reasoning.  
**Trade-offs**: Requires maintenance and provenance discipline; reduces repeated explanation and context fragmentation.  
**Sources**: [Apr 12](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2043347089438986609), [Jul 25](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2081020582728990734), [Aug 18](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2089716735146340481)

## Multiply-Yourself System

**When to use**: When expertise and demand are proven but the expert's labor is the bottleneck.  
**How**: Extract the causal playbook, select a capable operator, define standards and feedback, align compensation to measurable outcomes, and monitor supervision cost and cannibalization.  
**Trade-offs**: Revenue share aligns upside but adds attribution, quality, and competitive risk.  
**Sources**: [Mar 22](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2035707973259944013), [Jun 20](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2068279446713348479), [Aug 21](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2090916538257740013)
