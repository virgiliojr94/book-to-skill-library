# Decision Cheatsheet

## Fast Rules

| Situation | Default move | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nobody engages with your content | Make the benefit concrete; share solutions and expertise | Unknown creators have not earned interest in the journey itself | [Jan 2](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2007091103229448465) |
| You want a valuable network | Become excellent at something useful to the people you want to know | Reciprocal value precedes durable access | [May 1](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2050274937709977794) |
| You have never sold before | Promote a proven offer or sell a direct service | It isolates distribution from product risk | [Mar 11](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2031799206550081578) |
| You want a scalable product immediately | First gain industry depth, leads, and sales evidence | Hands-off formats add competition and unknowns | [Jun 6](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2063197143834243082) |
| You are unsure what AI skill to learn | Pick a useful or profitable problem and learn what it demands | Application selects relevant knowledge | [May 2](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2050596641019707536) |
| More study does not change your next action | Attempt the work and shorten the feedback loop | Preparation can become delayed failure | [Jun 19](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2067921418919063634) |
| A generic agent gives generic output | Add domain data, examples, an index, and operating rules | Specialized context is the differentiating layer | [Apr 7](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2041524052364329313) |
| You do not understand the process | Do not automate it yet | You cannot specify or evaluate the agent's work | [Apr 2](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2039705767113302260) |
| Your expertise is the bottleneck | Encode the method and enable a capable operator | Repeatable expertise can scale beyond labor | [Mar 22](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2035707973259944013) |

## Product Readiness Gate

- **No sales experience** → proven offer or service.
- **Some customers, unclear repeated pain** → keep delivering directly and record patterns.
- **Repeated pain, known buyer, working channel** → test a reusable product.
- **Proven expertise, repeatable delivery, labor bottleneck** → build a playbook and operator system.
- **Agent product with generic output** → strengthen domain context before adding model complexity.

## Tells and Smells

- “I need to learn everything first” → learning is detached from an active problem.
- “I will document the build and distribution will happen” → activity is being mistaken for buyer intent.
- “The agent will figure out the process” → missing process knowledge is being hidden by automation.
- “Only I can do this” → the causal playbook has not been extracted.
- “We will split revenue somehow” → attribution, standards, and downside are undefined.
