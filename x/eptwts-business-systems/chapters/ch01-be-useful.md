# Chapter 1: Be Useful

## Core Idea

Usefulness is the author's repeated base layer for income, audience growth, and relationships. The sequence is: acquire a real skill, create a concrete benefit, and communicate the solution so another person can use it. ([Jan 2](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2007091103229448465), [Aug 24](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2091925587434614963))

## Frameworks Introduced

### “Be useful”

Use this test when deciding what to learn, publish, offer, or bring to a relationship.

1. Name the person or group you want to help.
2. Identify a result they already care about.
3. Become good at a skill that can produce that result.
4. Share a solution, insight, or piece of expertise they can apply.
5. Let attention and relationships follow demonstrated value rather than asking for them first.

The author connects specialized competence to usefulness in March, usefulness to strong networks in May, and usefulness to payment, audience, and relationships in August. ([Mar 6](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2029857772511138281), [May 1](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2050274937709977794), [Aug 24](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2091925587434614963))

### Authoritative solutions over journey documentation

When you do not yet have a result people recognize, center content on the audience's benefit. Reframe personal experience as a transferable solution instead of assuming the journey itself is interesting. ([Jan 2](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2007091103229448465))

### Reciprocal networking

Do not approach networking as one-sided extraction. Become valuable enough that exchanging knowledge or access makes sense for both sides. ([Mar 17](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2033826211495833682))

## Mental Models

- **Value precedes attention**: use when tempted to optimize personal branding before building competence.
- **Benefit is the unit of content**: ask what the audience can do better after consuming it.
- **Relationships are exchanges**: ask what useful capability you bring before requesting help.

## Anti-patterns

- **Unearned journey content**: it asks strangers to care before giving them a reason. ([Jan 2](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2007091103229448465))
- **Networking as extraction**: asking capable people to invest in you without reciprocal value. ([Mar 17](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2033826211495833682))
- **Authority without underlying skill**: usefulness depends on getting good at something, not merely sounding certain. ([Mar 6](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2029857772511138281))

## Evolution and Boundary

The January post applies the rule to content. By July and August the author states it more broadly: improve your own life, make the improvement replicable for others, and use usefulness as the criterion for content, income, and relationships. ([Jul 24](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2080755939083260079), [Aug 24](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2091925587434614963))

## Key Takeaways

1. Build competence before performing authority.
2. Translate experience into a result another person can reproduce.
3. Make the benefit explicit in content and offers.
4. Bring value before asking for access or help.
5. Diagnose weak attention by checking usefulness before tactics.
