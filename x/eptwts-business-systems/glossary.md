# Glossary

**Application loop** — Attempt real work, expose the next knowledge gap, learn enough to clear it, and apply again. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2067921418919063634); Ch 3)

**Asymmetric bet** — A bounded venture whose transferable skills and relationships can retain value even if the venture itself fails. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2079838342628151586); Ch 3)

**Audience value** — A concrete benefit, solution, insight, or expertise the audience can apply; contrasted with centering an unknown creator's journey. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2007091103229448465); Ch 1)

**Data moat** — Domain-specific knowledge and examples that make a product using a generic model produce more specialized, dependable results. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2041524052364329313); Ch 4)

**Distribution apprenticeship** — Learning buying intent and acquisition through services or promotion of an already-proven offer before adding product risk. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2063197143834243082); Ch 2)

**Living knowledge base** — A maintained, indexed source of truth that agents can retrieve from and that compounds as work occurs. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2081020582728990734); Ch 4)

**Multiply yourself** — Encode proven expertise so other capable people can execute it, creating leverage tied to expertise rather than personal labor. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2068279446713348479); Ch 5)

**Problem-backed learning** — Acquiring a skill because a concrete useful or profitable problem demands it. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2050596641019707536); Ch 3)

**Proven offer** — A product or service with existing evidence of conversion, used to isolate and learn the distribution problem. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2015038599406784869); Ch 2)

**Revenue share** — Compensation based on a percentage of outcomes produced with another operator; useful only when incentives and attribution can be made clear. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2090916538257740013); Ch 5)

**Source of truth** — The central context agents consult before relying on broad model priors or fragmented conversation history. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2089716735146340481); Ch 4)

**Surface-area maxxing** — Running varied asymmetric experiments to accumulate skills, experiences, and connections that can transfer to later ventures. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2079838342628151586); Ch 3)

**Useful** — Capable of creating a concrete benefit for another person; the author's recurring prerequisite for income, attention, and reciprocal relationships. ([source](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2091925587434614963); Ch 1)
