## Core Frameworks and Mental Models

### 1. Be useful

Use usefulness as the common test for content, relationships, and offers. Get good at something that benefits another person, then communicate transferable solutions rather than centering an unearned personal journey. The author repeatedly connects usefulness to getting paid, earning attention, and building reciprocal relationships. ([Jan 2](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2007091103229448465), [Mar 17](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2033826211495833682), [Aug 24](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2091925587434614963))

Decision rule: if the intended audience cannot name the benefit they receive, strengthen the underlying expertise or make the result more concrete before optimizing presentation.

### 2. Forget about building; learn distribution first

For a beginner, avoid solving product and distribution at the same time. Start with a proven offer, then learn buying intent and distribution through affiliate promotion or direct services. Move toward reusable products only after gaining market knowledge, access to leads, and the ability to sell. ([Jan 24](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2015038599406784869), [Mar 11](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2031799206550081578), [Jun 6](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2063197143834243082), [Jul 24](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2080583304416309707))

This is sequencing, not a permanent rejection of products: later posts explicitly combine proven distribution with agent-native B2B software. ([Aug 19](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2090042832509620400))

### 3. Stop learning and start doing; surface-area maxxing

Learn against real problems, act quickly, and let market feedback select what deserves deeper study. Treat small asymmetric bets as skill-stacking opportunities: even a failed vehicle can produce transferable knowledge in distribution, copywriting, coding, design, or operations. ([May 2](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2050596641019707536), [Jun 19](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2067921418919063634), [Jul 22](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2079838342628151586))

The apparent tension with “keep learning like a beginner” resolves through application: keep learning, but attach learning to action and feedback. ([Jan 7](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2008906931671327172), [May 3](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2050900582232191413))

### 4. Data is the moat; give agents a living source of truth

Treat a generic model as raw capability. Domain data, indexed files, operating rules, and maintained context turn that capability into a dependable specialist. A living knowledge base should compound as work happens and give every agent a shared source of truth before it reasons or acts. ([Apr 7](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2041524052364329313), [Apr 12](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2043347089438986609), [Jul 25](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2081020582728990734), [Aug 18a](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2089715930024595939), [Aug 18b](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2089716735146340481))

Automation still requires process knowledge: understand the task and its boundaries before delegating it to an agent. ([Apr 2](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2039705767113302260))

### 5. Multiply yourself

After expertise has been earned through direct work, encode it into a playbook that enables capable operators. Align incentives by taking a percentage or revenue share when both sides improve with success. The leverage comes from repeatable expertise rather than adding more personal labor. ([Mar 22](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2035707973259944013), [Jun 20a](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2068279446713348479), [Jun 20b](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2068279861077025061), [Aug 21](https://x.com/eptwts/status/2090916538257740013))

## Decision Flow

1. **No concrete value yet?** Build a useful skill and solve real problems → [ch01](chapters/ch01-be-useful.md), [ch03](chapters/ch03-learn-by-doing.md).
2. **Useful skill but no reliable customers?** Sell a service or proven offer to learn distribution → [ch02](chapters/ch02-distribution-before-building.md).
3. **Using AI without consistent results?** Add domain context and a maintained source of truth; verify that the process is understood → [ch04](chapters/ch04-context-is-the-moat.md).
4. **Demand and expertise are repeatable?** Document the playbook, select operators, and align incentives → [ch05](chapters/ch05-multiply-yourself.md).
