# Ship complex things with AI

## Core Idea

AI removes the barrier to building ambitious products. The response is to increase ambition, not just speed.

## In his words

## What it says

The 2010s indie hacker response to new tools was incremental: faster SaaS, simpler landing pages. Levelsio argues the correct response to AI is to build products you previously could not — complex editors, hardware, tools that require weeks of traditional engineering. The AI does not just reduce the cost of what you already did; it enables a category of products that were previously out of reach.

The companion move: validate immediately. Add a Stripe button on day one. Avoid scope creep and premature optimization. Launch before you're comfortable.

## Anti-patterns

- Using "ship fast" as an excuse to avoid hard products. He means ship *complex* things fast, not ship trivial things fast.
- Ignoring the validation step. "Add a Stripe buy button immediately" is part of the framework, not an afterthought.
