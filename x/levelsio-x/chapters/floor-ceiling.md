# AI lowers floor, raises ceiling

## Core Idea

AI simultaneously enables non-technical people to build personal tools (floor drops) and makes experts dramatically more productive (ceiling rises). Result: crowded low end, high leverage at the top.

## In his words

> "But then I see my gf who also stopped using most SaaS and just asks Claude or ChatGPT whatever she wants/needs. And now even makes her own apps with Claude Code to solve problems she has (not even commercializing them, just for her own use). Aka the highly personalized and ephemeral interfaces we predicted AI would generate on the fly to solve whatever problem normies have. It's happening and it's here!"
> — [2026-07-26](https://x.com/levelsio/status/2081446515080417375) (quote of @lululi_lululi, endorsing)

> "AI raises the ceiling (indie hackers become far more productive) but also lowers the floor (the space is now crowded with normies who can vibe-code their own tools)."
> — [2026-07-30](https://x.com/levelsio/status/2082795824258359493) (quote of @fireship_dev, endorsing)

## What it says

The same tool that makes an expert ten times more productive also lets a non-technical person build a personal tool in minutes. The low end becomes saturated. The high end — where expertise, taste, and distribution compound — becomes more valuable. The implication: compete at the ceiling, not the floor.

## Anti-patterns

- Assuming the floor dropping means the ceiling drops too. The ceiling rises faster.
- Treating "normies building tools" as a threat. He presents it as context for where competition shifts, not as a problem to solve.
